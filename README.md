# Travel Research Guide

**Live site:** https://gjimzhou.github.io/travel-research-guide/

Public research notes distilled from prior ChatGPT travel-planning discussions.

This repository is **not** intended to grow into a general travel encyclopedia. A topic belongs here only if it was actually discussed first; web research may verify or source that discussion, but should not create unrelated new branches of content.

The repository intentionally excludes personal itineraries, booking details, home/work information, relationship events, and individual travel preferences. Useful conclusions are generalized into reusable notes.

## Research standard

Every addition should pass two tests:

1. **Conversation provenance** — the subject or conclusion was actually explored in prior user–ChatGPT discussion.
2. **Public-safe transformation** — personal itinerary details, identifying information, private correspondence, and individual preference profiles are removed or generalized.

External sources are used to verify discussed facts, especially time-sensitive rates, access rules, closures, cancellation terms, and transport details. They are not a mandate to expand into topics that were never discussed.

## Privacy rule

Do not add identifiable personal travel plans, booking numbers, home/work locations, relationship events, private correspondence, or an individual's preference profile.
