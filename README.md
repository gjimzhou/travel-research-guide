## Research standard

This repository separates two kinds of information:

1. **Durable framework** — decision rules such as minimizing hotel churn, comparing landed logistics cost, and valuing credits by avoided spend.
2. **Operational facts** — rates, road status, transfer fees, opening hours, maintenance, access rules, cancellation terms, and other facts that can change.

Operational facts should be dated and linked to an official or first-party source wherever practical. A dated note is a snapshot, not a permanent rule.

## Privacy rule

Do not add identifiable personal travel plans, booking numbers, home/work locations, relationship events, private correspondence, or an individual's preference profile. Convert useful lessons into general research conclusions instead.
