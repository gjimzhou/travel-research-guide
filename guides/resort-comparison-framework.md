# Luxury Resort Comparison Framework

This page distills themes that came up repeatedly in prior trip-planning discussions. It is not intended to become a general hotel-industry framework.

## Hotel quality and itinerary fit are separate questions

A resort can be excellent on its own and still be the wrong choice for a specific itinerary.

The recurring comparison dimensions were:

- whether the hotel itself is a meaningful part of the destination;
- room and resort quality;
- beach, pool, spa, golf, dining, and on-property activities;
- drive or transfer friction;
- how well the property fits the rest of the trip;
- cancellation flexibility;
- the real value of included credits and benefits.

## Minimize unnecessary hotel churn

Repeated one- or two-night moves often reduce trip quality by adding packing, checkout, transfer, and check-in time.

A split stay made sense in prior discussions only when the properties offered meaningfully different experiences, not simply because both were desirable hotels.

## Destination resort vs. base

Some hotels were evaluated as destinations in their own right: the stay should leave enough time for the property, dining, spa, beach, golf, or cultural programming.

Other hotels function mainly as a base for activities elsewhere.

That distinction affects how many nights the hotel deserves and whether adding another move improves or harms the trip.

## Credits should be valued by actual use

FHR, preferred-partner, and hotel credits were repeatedly compared by the spend they would actually replace.

A nominal credit is not worth face value if it pushes the guest into spending that would not otherwise happen.

## Re-plan after a disruption

Weather, course closures, transport problems, or an unavailable activity can change the logic of the entire itinerary.

The recurring lesson was to reconsider the schedule as a whole rather than mechanically replacing only the failed item.

## Scope

The purpose of this page is to preserve those previously discussed planning principles. New hotel-comparison dimensions should not be added here unless they have first been explored in conversation.
