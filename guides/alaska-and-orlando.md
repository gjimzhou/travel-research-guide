# Alaska and Orlando

## Alaska

The central structural choice is often **cruise vs. independent land itinerary**, not simply which hotel to book.

Independent travel gives more control over places such as Denali, Katmai, and aurora-oriented extensions, but demands more transport planning. Cruises simplify logistics and coastal access but constrain timing and inland depth.

Air routing should be included early in the decision because premium-cabin availability and international connections can materially change the attractiveness of an itinerary.

## Orlando

Orlando can be approached as more than a maximal theme-park checklist. Resort quality, golf, pool time, dining, and selective park visits can support a lower-friction trip.

Waldorf Astoria Orlando is a useful example of a base that supports both resort time and access to Disney-area activities and golf.
