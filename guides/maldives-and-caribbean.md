# Maldives and Caribbean

## Maldives

For top-end Maldives resorts, transfer overhead is substantial enough that very short stays can be inefficient. Split stays make the most sense when the properties offer genuinely different experiences and the total trip is long enough to absorb the transfer day.

Useful comparison dimensions include:

- seaplane vs. yacht or domestic-flight transfer;
- reef and snorkeling quality;
- villa privacy and orientation;
- beach quality;
- dining-plan structure;
- resort size;
- service model;
- points and elite-benefit economics;
- weather seasonality.

Waldorf Astoria Maldives Ithaafushi, Park Hyatt Maldives Hadahaa, and The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort illustrate materially different location, scale, and loyalty-program trade-offs.

## Caribbean

For short luxury escapes, transfer simplicity is unusually valuable. A nonstop flight followed by a short ground or boat transfer can create more usable vacation time than a theoretically superior resort requiring multiple connections.

Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton, Belmond, and comparable high-end island properties are best compared on total door-to-resort friction as well as room and beach quality.

## General lesson

For a short restorative trip, complexity is a cost rather than an achievement. Fewer well-chosen stops usually outperform island-count maximization.
